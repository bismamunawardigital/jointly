Jointly

Joint signatories live in hours, with every step on the record.

Jointly is an agent workbench for bank operations teams. The agent reads Finacle, SVS and EFR, builds the digital banking setup for a joint-signatory business, and a checker approves it with every source in view. This guide is how Jointly looks, moves and talks, so anyone can build a new screen without asking.

Who uses it

Customer Fulfillment checkers at UAE banks, on work laptops, all day.

What it feels like

A calm, exact instrument that knows your legacy-system day.

Languages

English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layouts.

01

Personality

Sara opens Finacle in one window, SVS in another, and a scanned mandate in a third. Jointly is the first tool in her day that doesn't make her hold things in her head. That's the feeling we design for.

Calm

Quiet surfaces. Colour shows up only when something needs her.

Exact

Real IDs, real amounts, real sources. Never "some records".

Accountable

Every claim links to where it came from. Every action has a name on it.

Warm

We know the legacy-system grind, and we say so like a good colleague would.

03

Colour

Neutrals carry about 90% of every screen. Blue means "you can act here". Green, amber and red mean data: verified, needs a person, blocked. A hue never does another hue's job. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Creek blue, primary scale

600 is the accent. 700 is hover and pressed. 50 is selected rows and the agent's soft tint. 900 is the only dark brand surface.

Warm neutrals

Semantic colour

Colour rules

RoleColourUsed forNever used for
InteractiveBluePrimary buttons, links, focus, active tour target, agent workingStatus, decoration, charts of outcomes
VerifiedGreenRules passed, read-back matched, approved, liveSuccess toasts for trivial actions
Needs a personAmberA human decision is waiting, an exception, an assumptionThings that are fine but different (name spellings)
BlockedRedRule failed, EFR failed, destructive actionValidation hints before the user tried anything

Known collision: the agent's blue and the primary button share a hue. We accept it because the agent's tint is always the soft 50 and the button is always solid 600. Text contrast: every ink on its soft background passes WCAG AA (4.5:1) in light and dark.

06

Motion

Motion tells Sara what just changed. It never decorates. Everything eases out, because things should arrive fast and settle slowly. Reduced motion turns it all off.

fast · 120ms

Hover, press, focus ring

base · 200ms

Tab change, pill change, toggle

slow · 320ms

Drawer, modal, new agent step

ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1). ease-in-out cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1), only for looping skeletons.
Enter

Fade in and move up 4px. Agent steps appear one by one, 200ms each.

Press

Buttons scale to 0.97. Feels physical, no bounce.

Never

Spinners for things that aren't running. Parallax. Confetti on approval.

12

Do's and don'ts

DoPut the source next to every claim the agent makes.
Don'tAsk Sara to remember something the system could show her.
DoShow what the agent didn't search, on the screen where she decides.
Don'tHide gaps in a separate tab.
DoUse amber only when a person has to decide.
Don'tColour harmless differences, like name spellings, as warnings.
DoUse mono and tabular digits for anything compared across systems.
Don'tUse gradients, glows, glass or gold.
DoGive one clear next step per screen.
Don'tShow the same button twice on one screen.
DoConfirm once before anything reaches a customer.
Don'tShow a spinner when nothing is running.
