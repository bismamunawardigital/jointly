    Hold for compliance

    The checker isn't sure and wants a second opinion. They pick a reason, the agent drafts the handover note with the evidence attached, and the case moves to "With compliance" in the left panel so nothing gets lost. Once it's sent, the case shows what happens next while compliance reviews it.
